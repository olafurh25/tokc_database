The Gathering

Unity in the Wildwood

Strategy Guide

The Gathering harnesses the power of nature and community. This faction excels at sustained growth, overwhelming opponents with sheer numbers and the raw power of the wilderness.

Key Strengths:

  • Growth: Deploy and rally effects build board presence over time
  • Resilience: Invulnerable and resilient traits provide staying power
  • Raw Power: High-strength followers dominate clashes
  • Synergy: Cards that support each other create compound advantages

Core Tactics:

  • Build early board presence with invulnerable followers
  • Use trader cards to maintain hand quality and flexibility
  • Leverage deploy effects to create overwhelming numbers
  • Protect high-value captains and heirs with supporting followers

Advanced Tips:

  • The Gathering rewards patience—build your engine before striking
  • Invulnerable followers create safe foundations for expansion
  • Time your captain deployments to maximize their impact
  • HQ cards multiply your advantages—prioritize their protection
