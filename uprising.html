The Uprising

Revolution from the Shadows

Strategy Guide

The Uprising fights through cunning, sabotage, and overwhelming numbers. This faction excels at disruption and asymmetric warfare, using clever tricks and coordinated strikes to topple even the mightiest opponents.

Key Strengths:

  • Disruption: Agent abilities and ruses disrupt opponent strategies
  • Numbers: Deploy abilities and recruitment create board presence
  • Flexibility: Trader cards and rally effects maintain hand advantage
  • Surprise: Night timing and deadly strikes catch opponents off-guard

Core Tactics:

  • Use The Irregulars and deploy cards to flood the board early
  • Leverage The Fence to maintain card economy with Rally
  • Time agent abilities during opponent's vulnerable moments
  • Coordinate cavalry flanks with deadly strikes for maximum impact

Advanced Tips:

  • The Uprising rewards careful timing—strike when enemies are overextended
  • Deploy effects create pressure that forces opponents to react
  • Use invulnerable followers to safely contest multiple regions
  • HQ cards amplify your board state—protect and leverage them wisely
