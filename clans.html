The Clans

Masters of the Northern Waves

Strategy Guide

The Clans, assembled on the margins of the kingdom, sail for its heart. Undertaking this great migration, a people of the steppes and sea. At their head is an heir of exiles carrying a great accord, inked by the many that sail in her wake, each pledged to her cause. Their banners of many colours snap in the wind as they aim their prows, arrow-true, towards the lands they lost long ago. Bright boats, full of songs and spears, bring means enough to start anew.

Key Strengths:

  • Mobility: Cavalry units and Pathfinder traits enable rapid repositioning
  • Resilience: Invulnerable followers provide reliable board presence
  • Tactical Surprise: Ruse cards and Deadly agents punish aggressive opponents
  • Recovery: New Havens Harbour brings supporters back from the Lost Pile

Core Tactics:

  • Use Shore Runner Sleds and Raid & Raze to flank into key regions
  • Leverage Wandering Embassy to move flanking units into councils for votes
  • Deploy Saga the Unifier with Rally for sustainable pressure
  • Time Nixing Knife's Deadly ability to eliminate high-value followers

Advanced Tips:

  • The Clans thrive on tempo—maintain constant movement to keep opponents reactive
  • Invulnerable followers can safely contest regions without fear of elimination
  • Riders of the North Sky generate influence when winning clashes, accelerating your economy
  • The Storyteller can steal Kingdom Cards by treating occupiers as strength 0—a devastating late-game play
