The Nobility

Heirs to the Ancient Throne

Strategy Guide

The Nobility commands through authority and legacy. This faction excels at political maneuvering, using votes, lore, and powerful champions to claim rightful dominion over the kingdom.

Key Strengths:

  • Political Power: High vote values dominate councils
  • Lore Acquisition: Efficient lore generation accelerates victory
  • Elite Units: High-strength captains and champions
  • Authority: Powerful command abilities control the battlefield

Core Tactics:

  • Prioritize council presence with high-vote cards
  • Balance lore acquisition with board control
  • Deploy champions strategically for maximum impact
  • Use trader cards to maintain card advantage

Advanced Tips:

  • The Nobility excels at the long game—establish dominance gradually
  • Vote efficiency matters more than raw strength in many situations
  • Protect your heirs and champions—they're your path to victory
  • HQ cards amplify your political and military advantages
