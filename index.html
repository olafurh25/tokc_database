Search Syntax Guide

Basic Search

ambush

Search for cards containing "ambush" anywhere in their text

"gain 1 influence"

Search for the exact phrase (use quotes for multi-word phrases)

Field-Specific Searches

name/n:petrichor

Search only in card names

text/txt:flank

Search only in card text (rules & commands)

type/t:tactic

Find all Tactic tiles (basic, advanced, kingdom, favour, tactic, faction)

faction/f:clans

Find cards from a specific faction (clans, uprising, nobility, gathering, aeronauts)

arch/a/archetype:heir

Find cards with a specific archetype (insert all archetypes here)

Numeric Searches

cost/c:3

Cards with exactly 3 cost

cost/c:2..4

Cards with cost between 2 and 4 (inclusive range)

strength/str:>=5

Cards with 5 or more strength (use >=, >, <=, <, =, !=)

votes/v:1

Cards with exactly 1 vote value

lore/l:2

Cards with exactly 2 lore value

Traits & Keywords

trait:invulnerable

Cards with the Item trait (item, companion, building, title)

Combining Searches

faction:clans type:advanced

Cards matching ALL conditions (implicit AND)

faction:clans OR faction:uprising

Cards matching ANY condition (use OR between terms)

text:draw -text:discard

Exclude terms with minus sign (cards with "draw" but not "discard")

(faction:clans OR faction:uprising) cost:3

Use parentheses to group conditions

Special Searches

release:basegame

Cards from the base game

release:"wild kingdom"

Cards from a specific expansion (aegis, aegis:nobility, aegis:aeronauts, etc.)

hq:true

Find only Headquarters cards

Advanced Examples

faction:clans cost:0..2 trait:pathfinder

Clans items with cost 0 to 2

(text:draw OR text:search) -type:tactic

Non-tactic cards that draw or search

strength:>=10 votes:0

High strength cards with no votes

faction:nobility arch:noble cost:3..5

Nobility nobles costing 3-5

Tips

• Searches aren't case-insensitive

• Fuzzy matching helps with typos (e.g., "millitia" finds "militia")

• Use quotes for exact multi-word phrases

• Spaces between terms mean AND (all must match)

• Field names can be shortened: str:5 (strength), arc:warrior (archetype)

• Numeric ranges use .. (double dots): cost:2..4
